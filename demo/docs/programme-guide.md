# Housing Support Programme — Guide

To be eligible for housing support you must be a current resident of the borough and have a household income below the published threshold.

Applicants may hold savings of up to £16,000 and still qualify for the programme.

The support office is open Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm, at the Civic Centre.

Advice sessions with a case worker last 45 minutes and can be booked by phone.

For general enquiries contact the programme team at enquiries@example.org.
